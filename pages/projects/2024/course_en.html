  Excel course  

In October 2023, as part of the "Cordées de la Réussite" program, we were given the task of teaching the basics of the Excel spreadsheet program to a class of 8th graders (4ème) at Jean Zay middle school (Niort, FRANCE) This outreach program helps students in order to build their ambition for school and overcome their lack of self-confidence.

To do this, we ran class sessions where students learned how a spreadsheet works and how to use Excel formulas. The final goal of this project was to organize a data visualization contest within the class, which means organizing data to make it easier to read quickly, for example by using plots.

The first session covered the basics of using a spreadsheet, teaching them how to make a table, do simple maths, and use Excel formulas through exercises and lessons. The last two sessions focused on building and reading plots, with practice exercises on interpreting them.

On the day of the data visualization contest, we came prepared with a topic and data on the success rate of the "brevet des collèges" (a French middle-school exam) in the Deux-Sèvres area (FRANCE). Students could ask us for help and advice throughout the contest if they had any troubles. After the contest, a small ceremony was held to present the best projects, and the winners received presents from the IUT of Niort (FRANCE).

Skills learned:

  • Improving my skills with Excel
  • Producing teaching materials for people new to computing
  • Learning the basics of teaching, from building an Excel course to presenting and teaching it in class
