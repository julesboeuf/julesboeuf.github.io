  Writing a report  

For this project, I had to write a statistical report to give an overview of the Seine-Saint-Denis department, covering employment/unemployment, and population. The data used for this project came from the INSEE website, and was used to show the department's situation. The report was entirely written in Word, and the measures were calculated in Excel.

Skills learned:

  • Using Excel and Word
  • Improving my writing skills
  • Understanding how to write and format a report
  • Finding data and calculating measures
