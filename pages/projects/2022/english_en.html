  Presenting an economic and cultural area in English  

This project involved presenting the city of Dijon (FRANCE), covering its economic side and then its cultural side, as an oral presentation, with the first part of the talk given in English. We did our research online, mainly on the city of Dijon's website to find information about its history and the major companies based there, as well as on the INSEE website to find population data and economic indicators for the city.

The presentation was made in PowerPoint, following the rules of an university presentation. For the English part, we relied on our own knowledge of the language, as well as help from our English teacher and an online translator when we could not find the translation of a word ourselves.

Skills learned:

  • Using PowerPoint
  • Finding information and data online
  • Improving my English skills
