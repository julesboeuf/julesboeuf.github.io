  Data visualization contest (Dataviz)  

The Dataviz contest involved producing a dashboard, using a tool of our choice, that answered a question we defined based on a given dataset. The contest was run with SNCF (the French national railway company). The dataset given to us was full of quantitative data for each train station, such as the number of travelers, the number of trips, the price per square meter in the cities where the stations are located, and the total population.

Our question was « what makes a train station attractive ? ». To answer this, we added another dataset to the information we already had: a dataset on accessibility equipment available in stations for people with reduced mobility. To measure how attractive a station was, we looked at how well equipped it was, the types of trains passing through it, and the number of passengers entering the station to travel.

The project was built using the Power BI tool. We had a full day to organize our question, decide on the charts we wanted to show, and write our comments on the topic.

Skills learned:

  • Using Power BI
  • Defining and interpreting a research question
  • Choosing charts to display data
